Services

Practical support, from eligibility to final delivery.

Four connected services — you can start with one, or combine them as your project grows. Every engagement begins with a written scope, price, and timeline before any work starts.

01Grant Writing

Grant Writing & Funding Strategy

Many consultants can identify programs or write applications. Fewer can understand the underlying research, challenge assumptions, structure the R&D, and translate complex work into language that funders find credible.

Program & funder matching

ACOA, NRC-IRAP, NBIF, Canadian Agricultural Partnership, NSERC, and provincial innovation programs.

Beyond research funding

Equipment, hiring, training, expansion, commercialization, export development, sustainability, and technology adoption.

Full application development

Eligibility interpretation, budget narrative, and a complete, reviewer-ready application.

Ongoing funding strategy

Repeat applications and funding monitoring for organizations that need this more than once.

02SR&ED

SR&ED Advisory & Documentation

Your technical work may qualify for SR&ED even if it doesn't look like "research" on the surface. We assess eligibility, structure the technical narrative, and build documentation that holds up under CRA review.

  • Eligibility assessment against real SR&ED criteria, not guesswork.
  • Technical narrative and evidence structuring for CRA submissions.
  • Annual SR&ED support for organizations that claim year over year.
03Data Analysis

Data Analysis & Impact Reporting

Raw project or research data doesn't speak for itself. It gets analyzed, interpreted, and turned into reporting that funders, partners, and your own team can act on.

Impact & outcome reporting

Built for funder reporting requirements and internal decision-making alike.

R&D & project data analysis

Credible analysis that supports the claims made in an application or report.

04Technical Writing

Scientific & Technical Writing

Complex R&D, methods, and results — translated into writing that funders, partners, and decision-makers who aren't specialists can actually follow and trust.
