05Common questions

What clients ask before they start.

What do I need to prepare for the first call?

Nothing formal. A short description of your project, a rough budget, and your timeline are enough to start. We'll work out the rest together.

Is my business or project eligible?

That's exactly what the free 20-minute consultation and follow-up eligibility assessment are for — we'll review your project against real program criteria before you commit to anything.

Can you guarantee approval?

No — funding decisions belong to funding agencies, not to me. What I commit to is an honest assessment, high-quality preparation, strong documentation, and full alignment with program requirements.

How much will it cost, and how do you charge?

A combination of fixed project fees, hourly consulting, and monthly retainers — with success-based fees considered for selected grants where appropriate. You'll always see the price before work starts.

Can you help with both the technical and financial sections?

Yes — including funding for equipment, hiring, training, expansion, commercialization, export development, sustainability, and technology adoption, not just research.
